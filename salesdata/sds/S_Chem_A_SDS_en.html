Harmful to aquatic life. Harmful to aquatic life with long lasting effects.

Precautionary Statements (P-Statements)

4. First-Aid Measures

Eye Contact: Immediately flush eyes with plenty of water for at least 15 minutes. Get medical attention if irritation persists. Skin Contact: Wash skin with plenty of water and soap. Get medical aid if irritation persists. Inhalation: Remove to fresh air. Give artificial respiration/oxygen if needed. Get medical aid. Ingestion: Do not induce vomiting unless directed by medical personnel. Get medical aid.

5. Fire-Fighting Measures

Extinguishing Media: Use water spray, dry chemical, carbon dioxide, or appropriate foam. Specific Hazards: Irritating and highly toxic gases (Carbon monoxide, Carbon dioxide) may be generated by thermal decomposition. Protective Equipment: Wear a self-contained breathing apparatus and full protective gear.

6. Accidental Release Measures

Personal Precautions: Use proper personal protective equipment (PPE) as indicated in Section 8. Environmental Precautions: Avoid release to the environment. Methods for Containment/Cleanup: Collect with absorbent material (liquid based on JP/FR/PL SDS). Avoid generating dusty conditions if spilled material dries.

7. Handling and Storage

Handling: Wash thoroughly after handling. Use with adequate ventilation. Avoid contact with eyes, skin, and clothing. Storage: Keep container closed when not in use. Store in a cool, dry, well-ventilated area.

8. Exposure Controls/Personal Protection

Engineering Controls: Use adequate ventilation. Install eyewash and body wash stations. Exposure Limits: Limited information available (e.g., CAS# 9003-04-7 (Netherlands): 0.05 mg/m³ MAC). Personal Protection: Hand: Appropriate protective gloves. Eye: Safety goggles. Skin: Protective clothing.

10. Stability and Reactivity

Stability: Stable under normal temperatures and pressures. Conditions to Avoid: Dust generation, excess heat. Incompatible Materials: Strong oxidizing agents. Hazardous Decomposition Products: Carbon monoxide, carbon dioxide, irritating and toxic fumes and gases.

11. Toxicological Information

Acute Toxicity (Oral): LD50 = 4600 mg/kg (mouse); LD50 = 2500 mg/kg (rat). Carcinogenicity: Not listed by ACGIH, IARC, or NTP. Potential Effects: May cause eye, skin, and respiratory tract irritation.

12. Ecological Information

Aquatic Toxicity (Fish): LC50 = 580-2000 mg/L (96 Hr, Bluegill/Sunfish). Aquatic Toxicity (Daphnia): EC50 = 168-280 mg/L (96 Hr). Ecotoxicity Note: Harmful to aquatic life with long lasting effects.
