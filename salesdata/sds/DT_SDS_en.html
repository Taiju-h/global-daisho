4. First-Aid Measures

  • Inhalation: Move to fresh air and keep at rest in a position comfortable for breathing. If feeling unwell, contact a doctor.
  • Skin Contact: Wash with plenty of water and soap. If feeling unwell, contact a doctor.
  • Eye Contact: Rinse cautiously with water for several minutes. Remove contact lenses, if present and easy to do. Continue rinsing. If eye irritation persists, get medical advice/attention.
  • Ingestion: Rinse mouth. Give plenty of water to drink. Get medical attention immediately.
  • Protection for First-Aiders: Wear appropriate protective equipment if necessary.

5. Fire-Fighting Measures

  • Extinguishing Media: Powder, Foam for water-soluble liquids, Carbon dioxide, Sand, Water spray.
  • Unsuitable Extinguishing Media: No information.
  • Specific Hazards: May emit irritating or toxic gases (e.g., Phosphorus oxides) during fire.
  • Specific Fire-Fighting: Cut off the combustion source and extinguish using appropriate media. The product itself does not burn.
  • Protection for Fire-Fighters: Wear appropriate protective equipment (gloves, glasses, mask, etc.).

6. Accidental Release Measures

  • Personal Precautions: Always wear protective equipment (gloves/glasses). Evacuate people to a safe area if large amount.
  • Environmental Precautions: Avoid release to the environment.
  • Cleanup Methods: For small amounts, absorb with sawdust, soil, sand, or rags. For large amounts, dike with earth to prevent flow and guide to a safe place for disposal.

7. Handling and Storage

  • Handling: Use appropriate exhaust ventilation. Wash hands thoroughly after handling.
  • Precautions: Wear PPE as needed. Avoid contact with eyes and skin.
  • Storage: Keep container tightly closed in a well-ventilated place. Store in a cool, dark place away from direct sunlight.

8. Exposure Controls / Personal Protection

  • Engineering Controls: Install eye washing and body washing facilities near the handling area. Use appropriate exhaust ventilation.
  • Exposure Limits: Not established.
  • PPE: Respiratory protection (as needed), Hand protection (Rubber gloves), Eye protection (Safety glasses), Skin protection (Long-sleeved work clothes).
