2. Hazards Identification

GHS Classification (Based on JP GHS Standard)

  • Physical Hazards: Not classified
  • Health Hazards: Not classified (Acute Toxicity, etc.)
  • Hazardous to the aquatic environment (Acute): Category 3
  • Hazardous to the aquatic environment (Chronic): Category 3

Hazard Statements

Harmful to aquatic life. Harmful to aquatic life with long lasting effects. May cause eye burns.

Precautionary Statements (P-Statements)

Prevention: Avoid release to the environment. Response: Immediately flush eyes with plenty of water. Storage: Keep container closed when not in use. Disposal: Dispose of contents/container according to local regulations.

4. First-Aid Measures

Eye Contact: Immediately flush eyes with plenty of water for at least 15 minutes. Get medical attention immediately. Skin Contact: Immediately flush skin with plenty of soap and water. Inhalation: Remove to fresh air. Ingestion: DO NOT INDUCE VOMITING. Give large quantities of water. Get medical attention immediately.

5. Fire-Fighting Measures

Fire Hazard: Not considered a fire hazard. Explosion Hazard: May explode when applied to red-hot aluminum. Extinguishing Media: Use any means suitable for extinguishing surrounding fire. Specific Hazards: May emit irritating or toxic gases (e.g., sulfur oxides) in case of fire.

6. Accidental Release Measures

Personal Precautions: Use proper personal protective equipment (PPE). Environmental Precautions: Avoid release to the environment. Methods for Containment/Cleanup: Vacuum or sweep up material and place into a suitable disposal container.

7. Handling and Storage

Handling: Use with adequate ventilation. Avoid breathing dust. Storage: Keep container closed when not in use. Store in a cool, dry, well-ventilated area away from incompatible substances.

8. Exposure Controls/Personal Protection

Engineering Controls: Use adequate ventilation. Exposure Limits: None set. Personal Protection: Hand: Appropriate protective gloves. Eye: Safety goggles. Respirators: Follow a respiratory protection program.
