4. First-Aid Measures

Eye Contact: Rinse cautiously with water for several minutes. Remove contact lenses, if present and easy to do. Continue rinsing. Skin Contact: Wash with plenty of soap and water. Inhalation: Remove to fresh air. Ingestion: Give large quantities of water. Get medical attention immediately.

5. Fire-Fighting Measures

Extinguishing Media: Use any means suitable for extinguishing surrounding fire. Specific Hazards: May emit irritating or toxic gases in case of fire. Protective Equipment: Wear appropriate protective gear.

6. Accidental Release Measures

Personal Precautions: Wear protective equipment (gloves, eye protection). Ensure adequate ventilation. Environmental Precautions: Avoid release to the environment. Methods for Containment/Cleanup: Collect small spills with absorbent material. Contain large spills with earth/sand and dispose of safely.

7. Handling and Storage

Handling: Use adequate exhaust ventilation. Wash hands thoroughly after handling. Storage: Store tightly closed in a cool, well-ventilated place.

8. Exposure Controls/Personal Protection

Engineering Controls: Install eyewash stations and body-washing facilities. Use appropriate exhaust ventilation. Exposure Limits: None set. Personal Protection: Hand: Rubber protective gloves. Eye: Protective glasses. Skin: Long-sleeved work clothing.
