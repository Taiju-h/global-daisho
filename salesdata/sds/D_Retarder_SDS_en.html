4. First-aid measures

InhalationMove to fresh air and keep comfortable for breathing. Seek medical attention if coughing, difficulty breathing or other symptoms occur.
Skin contactRemove adhering material and wash thoroughly with soap and water. Seek medical attention if skin symptoms persist.
Eye contactImmediately rinse cautiously with running water for several minutes. Remove contact lenses, if present and easy to do. Continue rinsing. Seek medical attention if irritation persists.
IngestionRinse mouth. Do not induce vomiting. Consult a physician. Never give anything by mouth to an unconscious person.

5. Fire-fighting measures

Suitable extinguishing mediaWater spray, foam, dry chemical or carbon dioxide, selected for the surrounding fire.
Fire-fighting precautionsAvoid dispersing the powder during fire-fighting.
Specific hazardsCombustion or thermal decomposition may release carbon monoxide and carbon dioxide.
Fire-fighting procedures and protectionFight from a safe upwind position and evacuate uninvolved personnel. Use suitable respiratory protection and protective clothing to avoid smoke exposure.

6. Accidental release measures

Personal precautionsVentilate the affected area and restrict access by unnecessary personnel. Wear protective equipment and minimize dust generation.
Environmental precautionsDo not release directly into drains, waterways or soil.
Containment and cleanupCollect using methods that minimize dust dispersion and place in a sealable container. Do not disperse using compressed air. Dispose of recovered material and wash water appropriately.

7. Handling and storage

Precautions for safe handlingMinimize dust when opening bags, charging equipment or transferring material. Work in a well-ventilated area. Avoid contact with eyes, skin and clothing. Follow Section 8.
Hygiene measuresDo not eat, drink or smoke in handling areas. Wash hands after handling and before eating or drinking.
Safe storage conditionsKeep containers tightly closed. Protect from direct sunlight, water, excessive heat and high humidity. Store separately from food and animal feed. See Section 10 for incompatible materials.
Packaging materialsPreferably retain the original container. If transferred, use a container of verified compatibility and label it with the product name.
