Bentonite Swelling Agent

Secures necessary viscosity immediately upon contact. Maximum volumetric boost stabilizes to over 90% of peak performance within 2 to 4 hours.

3. Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

1. Preparation: Fill mixer with water and start agitation.
2. Pre-addition: Add agent BEFORE bentonite.
3. Addition: Add bentonite to the pre-treated water.
4. Application: Ready for use after 10 mins of mixing.
