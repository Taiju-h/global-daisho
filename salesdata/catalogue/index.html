DEEPER 25 - maximum shield protection

Shock absorption

A special polymer forms an elastic-resilient "cushion" between the cutterhead face and the disc cutter ring.

This layer effectively absorbs and dampens impact energy when striking boulders or hard rock, preventing carbide ring cracking and spalling.

Extended cutter life

By dissipating impact energy, it dramatically reduces ring cracking and uneven wear patterns.

As a result, cutter change-out intervals are significantly extended, reducing downtime and overall operating costs.

DEEPER 25 - maximum shield protection

Mixed ground

The additive simultaneously prevents clogging in clays and absorbs shocks in hard layers.

It maintains stable excavation parameters even in highly variable mixed-face conditions, significantly reducing torque fluctuations and cutterhead vibration.

Extra protection

When sticky clays mix with hard boulders, unexpected cutter damage is common.

DEEPER 25 instantly forms a shock-absorbing lubricating layer that buffers peak "pin-loads", reducing metal fatigue and preventing catastrophic cutter failures.

RAD - rapid setting under any ground condition

Instant gelation

RAD causes instantaneous transition of cement grout from liquid to plastic-gel state directly inside the fracture, completely stopping outflow even in highly permeable and heavily fractured rock.

Complete void filling

Ensures 100% filling of the annulus around the bar or rock bolt, maximum bond strength and full load-bearing capacity - no material loss and no re-grouting required.

Excellent pumpability

Bentonite - reliable excavation stability and safety

Reliable wall support

Bentonite forms a high-viscosity, thixotropic slurry that effectively supports excavation walls (diaphragm walls, secant/contiguous piles), preventing soil collapse.

The trench remains fully stable throughout the entire construction phase.

Water control

Upon hydration, bentonite swells and creates a low-permeability filter-cake on the trench walls, effectively sealing against groundwater inflow and soil fluid loss.

Prevents soil loss and soil loss, protects fresh concrete from contamination and ensures long-term structural durability.

OPT JET - the new era of jet grouting

What is OPT Jet? An integrated system - not just a method.

OPT Jet is our proprietary jet grouting system that combines a unique double-fluid jet (ultra-high-pressure water + cement grout) with advanced chemical additives to maximise performance and quality.

Our high-performance jet grouting technology enables larger column diameters at lower lifting speeds, delivering significant time and cost savings.

With the OPT Jet system and OP-Flow chemistry we achieve larger column diameters, substantial cement savings and drastically reduced spoil volume, lowering disposal costs and improving site logistics.
