IMPORTANT: HERO BACKGROUND IMAGE

The hero-bg.jpg file is currently empty (placeholder).

After installing the theme:
1. Go to Appearance > Customize > Hero Section
2. Click "Hero Background Image"
3. Upload your own high-quality image (1920x1080px or larger recommended)

Recommended image specs:
- Resolution: 1920x1080px or higher
- Format: JPG or PNG
- File size: Under 500KB (compress if needed)
- Content: Professional, relevant to your business

Image tips:
- Use high contrast images for text readability
- Ensure important content isn't obscured
- Test on mobile devices
- Consider using a subtle overlay for better text visibility

You can find free stock images at:
- Unsplash.com
- Pexels.com
- Pixabay.com
