# 📋 Client Customization Checklist

Use this checklist to ensure you've customized all sections of your new website!

---

## 🎨 Colors & Styling

Go to: **Appearance > Customize > Colors & Styling**

- [ ] **Primary/Accent Color** - Change from yellow (#ffc107) to your brand color
- [ ] **Main Text Color** - Adjust if needed (default: #333333)
- [ ] **Button Text Color** - Color for text inside buttons (default: white #ffffff)
- [ ] **Font Family** - Choose from:
    - [ ] Inter (Default - Modern & Clean)
    - [ ] Roboto (Professional & Readable)
    - [ ] Open Sans (Friendly & Versatile)
    - [ ] Montserrat (Bold & Contemporary)
    - [ ] Poppins (Geometric & Modern)
    - [ ] Lato (Elegant & Warm)
    - [ ] Arial (Classic System Font)
- [ ] **Custom CSS** - Add any custom styling (optional, for advanced users)

---

## 🤖 AI Chatbot

Go to: **Appearance > Customize > AI Chatbot**

- [ ] **Enable AI Chatbot** - Turn on/off the chatbot widget
- [ ] **Chat Server URL** - Your Node.js server URL (e.g., http://your-domain.com:3001)
- [ ] **Agent Avatar Image** - Upload chatbot profile picture (square image, 200x200px recommended)
- [ ] **Chatbot Primary Color** - Color for chat bubble and messages

### Chatbot Setup Notes:

- You need a running Node.js server (see INSTALLATION.md for setup)
- Default color is blue (#2563eb) but you can change to match your brand
- The chatbot floats in the bottom-right corner
- Supports English and Japanese automatically

## 🎯 Quick Tips

### Icon Ideas for Services:

- Technology: 💻 🖥️ ⚙️ 🔧 ⚡
- Science: 🔬 🧪 🧬 ⚗️ 🔭
- Business: 📊 📈 💼 🏢 📋
- Innovation: 🚀 💡 ⭐ 🎯 🌟
- Environment: 🌍 🌱 ♻️ 🌿 💧

### Image Recommendations:

- **Hero Background**: 1920x1080px or larger, high quality
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **File Size**: Under 500KB for fast loading (compress if needed)

### Color Suggestions:

- **Professional Blue**: #0066cc
- **Modern Teal**: #20c997
- **Corporate Gray**: #6c757d
- **Vibrant Orange**: #fd7e14
- **Elegant Purple**: #6f42c1
- **Fresh Green**: #28a745

### Font Pairing Tips:

- **Modern & Tech**: Poppins or Montserrat
- **Professional**: Roboto or Lato
- **Clean & Minimal**: Inter or Open Sans
- **Classic**: Arial (system font - fastest loading)

## ✅ Final Steps

After customizing everything:

- [ ] **Preview** - Click "Preview" in Customizer to see changes
- [ ] **Publish** - Click "Publish" to save all changes
- [ ] **Test** - View your site in different browsers
- [ ] **Mobile Check** - View on phone/tablet
- [ ] **Backup** - Export your customizer settings (Recommended)

---

## 🆘 Need Help?

### Can't find something?

- Use the **search bar** in the Customizer
- Click through each section systematically
- Refer to INSTALLATION.md for detailed instructions

### Want to change something not listed?

- Use the **Custom CSS** field in Colors & Styling section
- Contact a WordPress developer for advanced modifications

### Save Your Work!

- Click **Publish** frequently to avoid losing changes
- Consider using a **staging site** for major changes
