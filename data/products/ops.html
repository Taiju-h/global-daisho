OP Flow Series

 

Additives for OPT Jet Method*

There are two types: the standard OP Flow S (OP1) and OP Flow L (OP2), which exhibits thixotropic properties and is effective as a liquefaction countermeasure.
By reducing the amount of cement slurry, both spoil volume and ground displacement can be minimized.


Applications

  • Chemical Grouting

  • Jet Grouting


    • Packaging
